Our story

The making of the hive.

At my Royal Mail leaving drinks, people kept asking about my exit plan. I hadn't even left yet. Here's how fifteen years of pop-up teams became SkillsHiveX.

Swipe each strip sideways to read every panel.

Read the story as text

Chapter 1: The Maverick

  1. 1. 13 years of saying yes Thirteen years in corporate life. I said yes to every opportunity. Every project. Every challenge. Solving problems, learning fast, taking on more.
  2. 2. The restructure Then came the restructure. The landscape changed. Uncertainty grew. And my autonomy, the thing I valued most, started to disappear.
  3. 3. The leaving drinks question At the leaving drinks, a colleague asked: "So, what's your exit plan?" The honest answer? I didn't really have one. But I knew I wanted the community to own their future, not some investor looking to extract value.

Chapter 2: The First Hive

  1. 4. The 2010 platform In 2010, I built a platform to bring skilled people together. A place to connect talent, ideas and opportunities.
  2. 5. A £20–30k investment I invested £20–30k to get it off the ground. But it didn't gain the traction I'd hoped for. It was a setback. Not a defeat.
  3. 6. Rethink and keep going So I stepped back, rethought the approach and kept going. The model was still strong; it just didn't need a complex platform. I could deliver it using everyday tools, focusing on people, partnerships and real-world collaboration.

Chapter 3: Assembling the Team

  1. 7. University of Greenwich We put the model into practice at UoG, creating pop-up teams to solve real challenges for businesses in the South East. It showed what was possible when the right people come together.
  2. 8. Alan joins Alan joined while completing his MBA, bringing experience, structure and a calm, steady perspective. A hugely important ally in the journey.
  3. 9. The Swarm Together we launched The Swarm, bringing students and diverse talent together to work on real projects. Fresh thinking. New perspectives. A true collaboration.
  4. 10. Take Your Shot (with Slinky and Sony) We created Take Your Shot with Slinky and Sony, giving emerging talent a platform to develop their skills and showcase their creativity.
  5. 11. A BAFTA nominee That journey led to an incredible moment: one of the talented individuals we supported went on to win awards and a BAFTA nomination.

Chapter 4: SkillsHiveX

  1. 12. 15 years of building Fifteen years of building, learning and adapting. Creating pop-up teams, connecting skills and helping great people do meaningful work together.
  2. 13. A community that stewards itself Today, SkillsHiveX is a community of people and teams who create value together. It's bigger than any one of us: a self-stewarding ecosystem that opens up opportunities and helps more people thrive.
  3. 14. Come and be part of it This is just the beginning. Come and be part of a growing community, where different skills, experiences and perspectives create real opportunities. A brighter together.
